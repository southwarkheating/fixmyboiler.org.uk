Emergency boiler repair

No heating, no hot water or water leaking? Book a priority visit and we aim to be with you within 2 hours.

Smell gas? This is a gas emergency. Leave the property, do not use switches or flames, and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. Book with us once it has been made safe.

How a priority visit works

  • Add £50 to a repair visit when you book. The total is £140, including the first hour.
  • We call you as soon as your booking comes in.
  • We aim to arrive within 2 hours of confirming, and will arrive within 4 hours except in exceptional circumstances, such as severe weather or road closures.
  • If we do not arrive within 4 hours for a reason within our control, we refund the £50 in full.
Book a priority visitCall 020 8050 2022

While you wait

  • Switch the boiler off at the fused spur if it is leaking.
  • Put a bowl under any drip and keep electrics dry.
  • Use electric heaters safely if you have them, and keep rooms closed to hold heat.
  • Note any fault code to tell the engineer.

For other heating and plumbing emergencies, see Southwark Heating emergency call-outs.

Questions

Do you offer 24-hour boiler repair?

Our priority visits run during working hours, and you can book them online at any time. For out-of-hours help, call 020 8050 2022 and we will tell you what we can do.

What counts as a boiler emergency?

No heating or hot water, especially with children, older or vulnerable people at home, or a leak. A gas smell is a gas emergency: call 0800 111 999 first.
