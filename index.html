How a repair visit works

No call-out charge on top, no surprises. You know the price before the engineer starts.

  1. Book in two minutes

    Pick a repair visit or a priority visit, choose a day and pay securely by card.

Quick answers

How much is a boiler repair?

A repair visit is £90, which covers diagnosis and up to the first hour on site. Extra time is £90 an hour, and parts are priced and agreed with you before we fit them.

Can you come today?

Repair visits can be booked for today. For the fastest response, choose a priority visit (+£50). We aim to be with you within 2 hours of confirming, and within 4 hours at the latest, or we refund the priority fee.

Which boilers do you repair?

All the main gas boiler makes, including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal, Baxi, Glow-worm, Viessmann, Potterton, Alpha and Intergas.

Is it worth repairing an old boiler?

Often, yes. If your boiler is very old, keeps breaking down or needs an expensive part, we will tell you honestly. You can see fixed prices for a new boiler on the Southwark Heating boiler finder.
